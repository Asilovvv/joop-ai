# Where the sales playbook's figures come from

The founder's playbook (`/owner/playbook`) quotes other people's numbers. They were collected on 4 November 2026
during the competitor and tactics research for the strategy document («Joop AI — стратегия с нуля»). Every figure
is the source's own claim, not something Joop measured; say "по их данным" when repeating one to a prospect.
Joop's own numbers come from the case page (`/owner/business/{id}/case`) once a business has run for a month.

| Figure in the playbook | Source (as recorded in the research notes) |
